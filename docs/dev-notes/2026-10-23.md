## Findings

- **311 delta growth rose about sevenfold on 2026-10-04 and has not come
  back.** Rows per run, from the `_runs` manifests:

  | Dates | Rows per run | Partition size |
  |---|---|---|
  | to 2026-10-01 | about 31k | about 10 MB |
  | 10-04, 10-06 to 10-11 | about 455k | about 148 MB |
  | 10-12 to 10-15 | about 32k | about 10 MB |
  | 10-16 to 10-23 | about 121k | about 36 MB |

  Watermarks advance one day per run, so this is upstream re-touching
  `:updated_at`, not the ingest re-reading. Whether the values changed is not
  measured. 10-02 and 10-05 wrote exactly 5,000 rows; that is harmless by
  design, since `watermark_out` is the last fetched row and the next run
  resumes there.
- **`raw_311_cases` is 907 MB and delta partitions are never prunable**, so the
  post-apply floor is about 1030 MB: 311 plus `building_permits` (15 MB) plus
  two copies of each snapshot dataset (about 103 MB). The 1 GB retention
  threshold now fires even right after an apply. Zone total 1595 MB.
- **An apply ran on 2026-10-12 with no dev note.** It is visible as the second
  ledger file, `_withdrawals/20261012T231324Z.json` (6 records, 33 in all), and
  as `business_locations` partitions starting at 10-11.
- **Retention has been red with exit 4 since 2026-10-05**; the proof passes
  (0 unproven on 10-19). Ingest and `dbt.yml` are green throughout.
- **Egress, 2026-10-01 to 10-22: about 76 GB** against the free 100 GB a month,
  rising from about 1.4 to 5.6 GB a day as the zone grew. October crosses the
  allowance around 10-27, and November is on course for over 200 GB if nothing
  changes. The 10-01 forecast of $0 with a weekly apply assumed the old 311
  rate.
