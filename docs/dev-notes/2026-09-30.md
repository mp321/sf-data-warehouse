## Findings

- **The zone is bounded again but only by someone running the apply.** About
  55 MB/day of growth, of which about 50 MB is another full copy of
  `business_locations`, whose freshness SLA (`stale_after_hours: 168`) is a
  week. The daily fetch buys six days of freshness nobody declared a need for
  and costs roughly four fifths of the zone's growth.

## Follow-ups

- **Fetch snapshot datasets on their SLA, not daily.** Needs an ADR: it changes
  ADR-19 step 2's "every run writes a complete partition" into "every run that
  writes one writes a complete one". Cuts growth from about 55 to about
  12 MB/day, which turns a weekly apply into one every eight weeks or so.
- **Then decide whether the apply may run unattended.** ADR-18 section 5
  refuses `--apply` in a workflow and says only an ADR may reopen it.
