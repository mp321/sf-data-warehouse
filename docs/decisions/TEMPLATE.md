# ADR-N. Short noun phrase naming the decision

Copy to `adr-<n>-<slug>.md` with the next free number. One ADR records one
decision; if you are writing "and also", that is a second ADR.

Be concise. One or two sentences per point unless the nuance is load bearing.

## Status note

`draft` while being argued. `active` once accepted, after which Decision and
Consequences are frozen. `superseded` when a later ADR replaces it: add the
replacement to `related` and change nothing else.

## Context

(The forces in play, and the constraint actually forcing the decision. Be
specific: a quota, an expiry, a cost, a deadline. No forcing constraint
usually means the decision is premature.)

## Options considered

**A. Name.** What it is, then the honest case against it. If you cannot write
a real case against your chosen option, you have not understood it yet.
